Mosimo Emblem: Three House (WIP)

A downloadable game

※開発者側としても非常に苦渋の決断ではありますが、このプロジェクトは将来的に日本語版への対応は完全に打ち切る予定です。

This is a work-in-progress demo for a Fire Emblem: Three Houses parody game. It aims for a simple yet mid-tier game level of quality.

As of 2025, production hit too very many roadblocks, crushing my motivation for 5 years. 

The scenario hasn't progressed much since the last version.

I know it feels like “Why now FE ThreeHouse?” but by the next release, many parts should be significantly improved from the previous demo. Recommended for those who haven't played the old one or want to try it now.

Prior knowledge of VIPRPG or the RTP isn't strictly necessary, but knowing both will enhance your enjoyment.

For detailed information, refer to the data from the 2021 Kouhaku Festival (unreleased).

https://w.atwiki.jp/vipkohaku2021/pages/124.html
